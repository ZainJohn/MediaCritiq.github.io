Our mission

Built for fans of novels that became unforgettable films.

MediaCritiq exists for people who love tracking a story from the page to the screen. We celebrate the books behind iconic films and help fans compare the adaptation choices, the emotional shifts, and the lasting impact of each version.

What we do

We help readers and viewers discover the stories behind their favorite adaptations, making it easier to move from the film back to the original novel and understand what changed and why.

Why it matters

  • Find the original novel behind a memorable film
  • Compare adaptation choices with the source story
  • Discover new favorites in one place
  • Enjoy a premium streaming-style browsing experience
